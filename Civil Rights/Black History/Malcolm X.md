He was born in May 19th of 1925 in Omaha, Nebraska. He was the son of ==**Earl little**== and ==**Louise Langdon**==, but ultimately did not take their names as their last names were of slave owner origin, as was custom back then for slaves to inherit their owners names. The "X" is a statement to him being his own man, free of any slave connotation. His parents were admirers of the ==**Pan-African activist[[Garveyism|Marcus Garvey]]**==, who was a Jamaican political activist and founder of UNIA-ACL. His family was often harassed by members of the Black Legion, an offshoot of the KKK, and 4 of his uncles were murdered by white racists.
