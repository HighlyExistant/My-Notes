Started by the first national hero of Jamaica, Marcus Mosiah Garvey, a Jamaican Muslim, his ideology was one of **racial redemption**. His ideas set the catalyst for the idea that ==**Black is Beautiful**== and ==**Black Power**== calls and programs that occurred in the 1960s. Important to note that the ==**he was not advocating for integration**== but instead for them ==**to be proud of their African past and build their own civilization**==. The main idea is that Africans have been exploited and dehumanized more than any other race or class in the history of humanity, and that together they can attain total liberation for all Africans, basically ==**Back to Africa**==, as it was called. 

> _A people without the knowledge of their past history, origin and culture is like a tree without roots._  
> 
> _If you have no confidence in self, you are twice defeated in the race of life._ 
> 
> _Africa for the Africans… at home and abroad!_ - Marcus Mosiah Garvey

The ideology was so that black people could help other black people further their prosperity through cooperatism, such as black people supporting black owned businesses.
> *Garveyism has taught that political power without economic power is worthless. - Francis E. Dorsey*

# The UNIA
![[UNIA-Flag.png]]
The flag was adopted in the 1920s stating that the colors of the black race are red, black and green. The lack of a symbol for black people was "the basis of outright racism towards Black people." It's meaning is in a ==**This Flag of Mine by Amy Jacques Garvey**==. The UNIA has inspired people such as:
* Kwame Nkrumah
* Jomo Kenyatta
* Nnamdi Azikiwe
* Bob Marley
* Malcolm X
* MLK Jr
* W. E. B. Du Bois (William Edward Burghardt Du Bois)
## This Flag of Mine
> **Regardless of what is told of it; Here’s to this flag of mine; The Red, Black and Green; Hopes in its future bright, Africa has seen.**
> 
> Here’s to the Red of it;  Great Nations shall know of it; In time to come; Red blood shall flow of it; Great flag of mine.
> 
> Here’s to the Black of it; Four Hundred millions back of it; Whose destiny depends on it; The RED, BLACK, and GREEN of it; Oh, Flag of mine.
> 
> Here’s to the Green of it; Young men shall dream of it; Face shot and shells of it; Waving so high.
> 
> Here’s to the whole of it; Colors broad and pole of it; Pleased is my soul with it; Regardless of what is told of it; Thank God for giving it; Great Flag of Mine.
