# Atomic Mass
The unified atomic mass unit is what we use to denote the mass of individual atoms. It is denoted as $u$ and is approximately $1.660540 \times 10^{-27} \text{ kg}$. 
1. ==**Proton**==: $\approx 1.007 u$.
2. ==**Neutron**==: $\approx 1.008 u$.
3. ==**Electron**==: $\approx 0.00055 u$
## Conversion using Avogadro's Number
We say that a mole is $6.02214076\times 10^{23}$, which is the amount of a certain substance (atoms), to get a mole. A formula unit consists of a substances molar mass times Avogadro's number.
