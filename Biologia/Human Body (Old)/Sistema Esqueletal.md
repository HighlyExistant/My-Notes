# Hueso Largo
## Medula Osea Amarilla
Dentro de la cavidad medular, aqui se almacena la grasa.
## Production of Blood
Blood is created in la medula osea roja en el hueso largo. Aqui se crean los tres tipos de sangre
* Plaquetas
* Globulos Rojos
* Leucocitos (Globulos Blancos)
## Esqueleto Axial
Contains the middle part of the skeleton, including:
* The Skull
* Rib cage
* Vertebral Column (Below the rib cage, following the spine)
* Ossicles (or the inner ear)
* Hyoid bone (connecting the rib cage and skull)
