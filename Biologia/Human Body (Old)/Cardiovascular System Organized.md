## Parts of the Heart
### Atrios
Camaras superiores pequeñas, de paredes finas. Reciben sangre de distintas partes del cuerpo.
1. ==**Derecho**==: Recibe sangre de todo el cuerpo, excepto el pulmon. Llega por la vena cava superior e inferior.
2. ==**Izquierdo**==: Recibe sangre del pulmon a traves de las venas pulmonares.
### Ventriculos
* ==**Derecho**==: Bombea sangre hacia el pulmon a traves de la arteria pulmonar
* ==**Izquierdo**==: Bombea sangre hacia el resto del cuerpo a traves de la arteria aorta.
# My Analogy
* The Cardiovascular system is composed of pipes, and processes which each pertain to one thing: <mark style="background: #ADCCFFA6;">Input</mark> and <mark style="background: #FF5582A6;">Output</mark>.
* <mark style="background: #ADCCFFA6;">Veins</mark> are the input pipe $\mathbf{V}$. Carries blood without oxygen into the heart, where it goes through the process of filling it with oxygen. $\mathbf{H_I}: \mathbf{V} \to \mathbf{V}\cup \mathbf{C}$. The main ones are ==**la vena cava superior e inferior**== (Atrio Derecho). And then theres the pulmonary veins 
* <mark style="background: #FF5582A6;">Arteries</mark> are the output pipe $\mathbf{A}$. It travels via the function $\mathbf{H_O}: \mathbf{A}\to \mathbf{A} \cup \mathbf{C}$. The main output pipes are ==**Ventriculos**==: "La Aorta" which outputs blood to the body, and ==**Ventricula Derecha**== which pumps blood to the lungs. 
* <mark style="background: #F7A51C;">Capilaries</mark>: Serve as a shared space, so that the arteries and veins can talk amongst each other $\mathbf{C}$. They are delicate, and are in charge of delivering nutrients and oxygen throughout your body ^d12f6d
* <mark style="background: #971f21;">Hemoglobina</mark>: Their job is to transport oxygen across the blood, specifically 4 molecules of $O_2$ for each molecule of Hemoglobina.
* Talking about all the transformations that can occur are: $$\mathbf{H_I}$$
* There are then two functions dedicated to pumping blood in the heart this: $$\mathbf{H_D}(b):=\text{Relaxing, Diastole}$$$$\mathbf{H_S}(b):=\text{Contraction, Sistole}$$Where $\mathbf{H}_D$ denotes the input of blood unto a heart $\mathbf{H}$, while $\mathbf{H_S}$ denotes output unto a heart $\mathbf{H}$.
