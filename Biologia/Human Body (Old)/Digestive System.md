# Definitions
* ==**Digestion**==: Is the degradation of insoluble macromolecules of nutrients via hydrolysis, which produces smaller molecules which are soluble.
* ==**Ingestion**==: The introduction of nutrients into the body through some mechanical process.
* ==**Peristalsis**==: Rhythmic wave of muscular contractions which move something along the digestive tract. Occurs in the esophagus.
# Digestive Process
* ==**Digestion of carbohydrates**== starts in the mouth, and is further helped by the pancreas.
* ==**Digestion of proteins**== starts in the stomach.
* ==**Emulsification de Fatty Acids (Grasas)**==: Caused by the Bilis produced in the liver.
* ==**Digestion of Fatty Acids**==: Occurs only in the Pancreas
* ==**Filtering of alcohol and excess Glucose/Amino Acids/Vitamin A's**==: Occurs in the Liver
## Liver (Hígado)
### Functions
1. ==**Bilis**==: Produced in the Liver, contienen sales biliares cuales su propósito es emulsificar las grasas en gotitas.
2. ==**Filtration**==: Filters blood and incorporates it into the circulatory system:
	1. Separates strange substances, such as alcohol
	2. Separates excess:
		* Glucosa $\to$ Glucogeno
		* Amino Acidos $\to$ Urea
		* Vitamina A $\to$ Almacena
## Pancreas
* ==**Amilasa**==: Produced in the Pancreas, helps digest carbohydrates.
* ==**Pancreatic Juice**==: Composed of an alkaline liquid and various enzymes, which are: 
	* ==**Lipasa**==: Helps digest fats, and is the only enzyme that helps with processing fats
	* ==**Tripsina**==: Digests Proteins
	* ==**Amilasa**==: Digests Carbohydrates
	* ==**Nucleasa**==: Digests Nucleotides
* ==**Horomonas**==
	* Secretina
	* Colecistocinina
	* Pancreocimina
	* Gastrina
* ==**Insulin**==: Produced in the Pancreas via ==**Beta Cells**== to reduce the levels of sugar, while ==**Alpha Cells**== make hormones.
# Vellocidad
## Microvellocidades
Aqui es donde termina el proceso de digestion.
### Enzimas
* Peptidasas: Help with the digestion of proteins
* Maltosa: Helps with the digestion of glucose
* Lactose: Helps with the digestion of glucose and galactose.
==**No tiene Lipasa para digerir grasas**==
### Absorption
Ocurre en los capilares sanguineous y los capilares linfáticos.
* Vellocidad $\to$ Higado $\to$ Higado Filtro $\to$ Vena, Cava $\to$ Cava inferior $\to$ Corazon (auricula derecha)
* Capilar linfatico $\to$ vaso linfatico $\to$ conducto toraxico $\to$ vena subclavia izquierda $\to$ Vena cava $\to$ Corazon (auricula derecha)
==**Todo lo que nosotros absorbemos en las vellosidades va a el corazon en la auricula derecha**==.
