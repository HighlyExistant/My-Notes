# History
## Jean Baptiste de Lamark (1744-1829)
this is the first scientist to propose a theory of evolution, wherein animals would evolve over time to achieve different results, using the giraffe as an example.
## Charles Darwin (1809-1882)
After travelling, they observed that the birds from the Galapagos were similar to those in South America. They then went on to study the work of agriculturists and farmers, to later propose his theory of ==**natural selection**==, where favorable characteristics of animals hitherto the environment they are in, wherein they will change over time to mutate to accommodate to their environment.

In a similar branch, there is ==**artificial selection**==, moved along by humans, in which they selectively breed things (e.g. Dogs or food items like vegetables) to something they favor.
