* <mark style="background: #FF5582A6;">Monomer</mark>: Small organic molecules used as subunits to construct Polymers.
* <mark style="background: #D2B3FFA6;">Polymer</mark>: Large organic molecules composed of monomers.
## Standard Molecules
* ==**Carbon Dioxide**== $=\text{CO}_2$
* ==**Water**== $=\text{H}_2\text{O}$
## Sugars (Carbohydrate)
These contain Carbon, Hydrogen and Oxygen with a proportionality of 1:2:1 respectively.
* <mark style="background: #FF5582A6;">Monosaccharides</mark>: The monomers of sugars.
* <mark style="background: #FF5582A6;">Glucose</mark> $=\text{C}_6\text{H}_{12}\text{O}_6$
* <mark style="background: #FF5582A6;">Ribose</mark> $= \text{C}_5\text{H}_{10}\text{O}_5$
* <mark style="background: #D2B3FFA6;">Polysaccharides</mark>: The polymers of sugars
## Lipids (Fats)
These organic molecules also contain Carbon, Hydrogen and Oxygen, except they have a much smaller proportion of Oxygen in comparison to Carbohydrates. These are split up in two parts
* <mark style="background: #FFB86CA6;">Saturated Fats</mark>
	* Individual bonds.
	* ==**Solid at room temperatures**==
* <mark style="background: #FFF3A3A6;">Unsaturated Fats</mark>
	* One or more double bonds.
### Phospholipids
These are made up of a:
* ==**Phosphate Group**==: $PO_4^{3-}$ These structures are hydrophilic due to their negative charge making them ions.
* ==**Carboxylic Acids**==: $R$-$CO_2H$ The $R$ in the carboxylic acid is called an $R$ chain, where $R$ is referring to an our ==**carbon chain**== includes:
	* ==**Hydrocarbons**==: This group in specific depending on whether it is unsaturated or unsaturated has a different formula
	* <mark style="background: #FFB86CA6;">Acyclic Saturated Hydrocarbon</mark>: $C_nH_{2n+2}$ This means there are no ring structures. For each Carbon atom it is paired with two hydrogen atoms. The extra two hydrogen atoms are given at the front and end of the structure.
	* <mark style="background: #FFB86CA6;">Saturated Fatty Acids</mark>: $C_nH_{2n}O_2$.
	* <mark style="background: #FFF3A3A6;">Single Double Bonded Unsaturated Hydrocarbons</mark>: $C_nH_{2n}$. The reason this lacks the two extra hydrogen atoms is because the each carbon atom in the double bond uses 1 hydrogen atom each, getting rid of 2 hydrogen atoms that would've been there.
	* <mark style="background: #FFF3A3A6;">Unsaturated Fatty Acids</mark>: The formula for these is varied. It can be: $CH_3(CH_2)_nCH$ and or $CH(CH_2)_nCOOH$.
* ==**Glycerol**==: $C_3H_8O_3$ these are what unites fatty acids into ==**triglycerides**==.
## Nucleotides
Compound consisting of nucleoside linked to a phosphate group.
* ==**Adenine**==: $\text{C}_5\text{H}_5\text{N}_5$
* ==**Adenosine**== $=\text{C}_{10}\text{H}_{13}\text{N}_5\text{O}_4$
* $mRNA$: Lleva la información para construir proteínas a los ribosomas
* $tRNA$: Lleva los aminoácidos que se usarían para la traducción.
* $AUG$: Codon iniciador, dice cuando comienza la ADN.
* $UAA,UAG,UGA$: Codon terminador, dice cuando termina la ADN.$
* ==**Telomere**==: $TTAGGG$-$3$, tells the cell when to do [[Cellular Reproduction|cell division]].  ^1a597f
### Nucleotide Triphosphates
* ==**Adenosine Triphosphate (ATP)**== $= \text{C}_{10}\text{H}_{16}\text{N}_{5}\text{O}_{13}\text{P}_{3}$ 
### Dinucleotides
* ==**Nicotinamide Adenine Dinucleotide Phosphate (NADPH)**== $=\text{C}_{21}\text{H}_{29}\text{N}_{7}\text{O}_{17}\text{P}_{3}$ is the ==reduced agent==.
* NADP$^{+}$ is the ==oxidized agent== $= \text{C}_{21}\text{H}_{28}\text{N}_{7}\text{O}_{17}\text{P}_{3}$
