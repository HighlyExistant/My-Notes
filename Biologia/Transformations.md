 * ==**Photosynthesis**==: $6\text{CO}_2 + 6\text{H}_2\text{O} \to \text{C}_6\text{H}_{12}\text{O}_6 + 6\text{O}_2$ which can also be written as $6\text{ Molecules of Carbon Dioxide }+ 6\text{ Molecules of Water } \to \text{Glucose and }6 \text{ Oxygen molecules}$
	 * ==**Light Dependent**==: $\text{Light }+ 6\text{H}_2\text{O} \to \text{ATP}+\text{NADPH}$
	 * ==**Light Independent (Calvin Cycle)**==: $\text{ATP} + \text{NADPH} + 6\text{CO}_2 \to \text{C}_6\text{H}_{12}\text{O}_6$
* ==**Transcripción**==: $\text{ADN} \to \text{mRNA}$
* ==**Traducción**==: $\text{mRNA} \to \text{proteina}$
