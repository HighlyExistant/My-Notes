# Secreción
La liberación de sustancias que el cuerpo necesita para llevar diferentes procesos metabólicos, tales como:
1. Enzimas
2. Hormonas
3. Mucosidad
## Process
1. The [[Parts of a Cell#Golgi Apparatus|Golgi Apparatus]] processes secretory [[Parts of a Cell#Vesicles|vesicles]] from the ER.
2. These secretory vesicles are released through [[Membrane Transportation#Exocytosis|exocytosis]].
## Diferencias entre Defecación
Mientras que la excreción es la eliminación de desechos metabólicos, defecación es la expulsion de residuos no absorbidos atreves del recto hacia el ano.
# Orina
La orina es el producto final de la excrecion. Contiene:
* Urea
* Acido Urico
* Amonia
* Sales
* Toxinas
* Hormonas
# Kidney (Riñon)
Is responsible for filtering blood and excreting waste products in urine. It's divided between two regions
1. ==**Cortex**==: Outer Region
2. ==**Medulla**==: Inner Region
Blood enters the renal artery, is filtered, then exits through the renal vein.
## Nephron
There are around 1 million nephrons in each kidney, which filters blood and forms urine. The processes that the nephron undergoes are:
1. ==**Filtration**==: The process occurs in the ==**renal corpuscle**== (corpuscle is Latin for small body).
	1. the ==**glomerulus**== which composes a network of [[Cardiovascular System Organized#^d12f6d|capillaries]], will filter fluid and ions from the blood.
	2. Afterwards it will pass into the ==**Glomerulus capsule**==, (Sometimes called Bowmans Capsule) where fluid filtration begins passing through the nephron.
2. ==**Reabsorption**==: Occurs in the 
	1. ==**proximal convoluted tubule**== (PCT) (tubo convoluto proximal). It reabsorbs nutrients like water, and electrolytes into the bloodstream, while secreting waste like hydrogen ions and ammonia.
	2. ==**Loop of Henle**==: Extends to the medulla where the descending limb is permeable to water concentrates the filtrate, and reabsorbs water. the ascending limb is impermeable to water and reabsorbs sodium, potassium and chloride.
