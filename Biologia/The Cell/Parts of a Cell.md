# Definitions
* ==**Organelle**==: Specialized organic structures within a living cell. 
# Vesicles
Is an ==**organelle**==, consisting of liquid or ==**cytoplasm**== enclosed by a lipid bilayer. The etymology of the word comes from the Latin word vesicular, for "bladder" or "blister". Their primary function is storage and transport.
## Lysosome
Is a ==**vesicle**==. It comes from the word ==**Lyso**==, meaning digest in Greek, and ==**soma**== which means body. Its purpose is to digest foreign invaders, like bacteria. It also digests the entire cell, upon the cells death. In reality, a lysosome is simply a vesicle which contains within it a Hydrolytic enzyme ([[Enzymes#Hydrolase|Hydrolase]]).
1. ==**Heterophagy**==: If a foreign invader attacks the cell, the lysosome will destroy it. (==**Hetero**== = Other, ==**Phagy**== = Eating)
2. ==**Autophagy**==: If an organelle is not working properly within the cell a lysosome will destroy it. (==**Auto**== = Self, ==**Phagy**== = Eating)
3. ==**Apoptosis**== (Programmed Cell Death): If the cell is too infected with bacteria, the lysosome will burst and destroy the cell. ==**From the Greek word meaning "falling off"**==.
This is to say that the lysosome is a coldblooded killer, who wouldn't bat an eye at getting the job done. 
## Vacuole
Is a ==**vesicle**==. It's main function is to work as either a storage unit or a place to put the waste of the cell.
### Central Vacuole (Plant Cells)
These are only contained in the plant cells. They usually hold large amounts of water ($H_2O$), but can also hold other things like pigments, which give the plant its color, ions and waste products. The walls of the vacuole are known as the ==**tonoplast**==, as the larger it is, the more tension it will put on the plant cell. 
### Phagosome
Also known as phagocytic vesicles. Contains a particle that has undergone phagocytosis, to later be eaten by the lysosome.
### Phagolysosome
Is the fusion between a [[Parts of a Cell#Lysosome|lysosome]] and a [[Parts of a Cell#Phagosome|phagosome]], which is responsible for degrading foreign materials to preserve homeostasis.

A phagolysosome containing food for the cell, is known as a food vacuole.
### Pinosomes
Also known as pinocytic vesicles. Contains a particle that has undergone pinocytosis.

# Golgi Apparatus
Proteins from the ER are processed into vesicles such as ==**lysosomes**== and ==**vacuoles**==.
