# Savanna
Located in the tropic and subtropic regions -, in which vegetation is characterized by wide pastures and some trees.
# Grasslands
Located in temperate climates, these are located in every continent except Antarctica.
# Temperate Forests
Composed of trees with relatively high humidity.
# Tundra
Located in cold climates where it is permanently frozen. These prevent the roots of plants to penetrate a lot, and that large trees exist. Instead vegetation is small. The permafrost that exists here can be home to lots of prehistoric fossils as well as harmful gas emisions.
# Taiga
Large forests of "coníferos".
# Deserts
Characterized by minimal rain and extreme temperatures. The fauna is primarily small.
