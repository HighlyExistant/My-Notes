(a) if $x \in X$ and $y \in X$ there is a unique $z \in X$ such that $x + z = y$.
* $X$ is a vector space
* $x + z=y$ 
* We know that for each $x \in X$ there exists a $-x \in X$ such that $x + -x = 0$.
* We also know that for any $y \in X$ $y + 0 = y$ therefore when $z = (-x + y)$ then $x + z = y$ because $x + (-x + y) = y$.
