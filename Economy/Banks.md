## How to Create Money
Banks contain only a fraction of the money that people deposit into them. With this money they invest in other projects in hopes of making a profit. When someone comes to the bank asking for extra money, that is a loan, ==**they simply increase the number of money that person has**==. ==**Another way is by buying or selling bonuses**==.
## Largest Loan a Bank can make
* Is equal to its excess of reserve.
## Interest rate from one bank to another
* Tasa de fondos federales (federal funds rate).
## Interest rate from FED to a bank
* Tasa de descuento (Discount Rate)
## Interest rate from the Banks to their best clients
* Tasa primo (prime interest rate)
