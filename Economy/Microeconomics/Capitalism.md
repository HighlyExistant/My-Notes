# What is Capitalism
System of free market, wherein people have access to private property, which we can refer to as capital. This concept of private property gives incentives to people to invest, to increase the value of the capital. For this system of free market to work, you need to be able to choose between different corporations (against monopolies), so that you can choose which companies are the best. Without a choice between companies, you don't have competitors and therefore companies have no incentives to give you a better service.
## Price as a coordinator
Those who produce consumables, will respond to increases in product pricing, as:
* higher price $\Rightarrow$ less sails $\Rightarrow$ less production
* lower price $\Rightarrow$ higher sails $\Rightarrow$ higher production
## Incentives of Capitalism
This system is ==**supposed to**== cause producers to create products more efficiently, and advance technology to compete in the market.
## Government Involvement
In a free market, the government can still be active, but it is limited. It's responsibilities include but are not limited to:
1. Offering national security
2. Offering security and protection to properties
3. Offering systems of justice for discrepancies
4. Offering public or quasi-public goods.
