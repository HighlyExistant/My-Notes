# Definitions
1. ==**Fixed Cost**==: Does not change with the level of production
2. ==**Variable Cost**==: Changes with the level of production.
3. ==**Total Cost**== = Fixed Cost + Variable Cost.
4. ==**Average Cost**==: [[What is Probability and Statistics#Mean or Average|Go figure]].
# Short term Cost
The total cost divided by the level of production (Supply) $Q$, also represents the average total cost: $$\frac{\text{Total Cost}}{Q}$$
While the marginal cost: $$\frac{\Delta\text{Total Cost}}{\Delta Q}=\frac{dP}{dQ}$$
