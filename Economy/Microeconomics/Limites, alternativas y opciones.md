# The Law of Supply and Demand
## Law of Demand
This law simply states that the demand of an object and it's price have an inverse relation
1. Lower prices $\Rightarrow$ Higher demand
2. Higher prices $\Rightarrow$ Lower demand
Intuitively you can think of a person not wanting to buy as much, since they either don't have enough money, or want to save it for when the prices lower, e.g. lower demand.
## Law of Supply
This law simply states that the price and supply of an object are a direct relation.
1. Lower prices $\Rightarrow$ Lower supply
2. Higher prices $\Rightarrow$ Higher supply
Intuitively you can think of a person not wanting to buy as much, since they either don't have enough money, or want to save it for when the prices lower, leading to supply not selling and augmenting.
