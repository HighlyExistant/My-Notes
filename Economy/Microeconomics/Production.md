When we talk about the costs of production, we are either talking about long term or short term costs. The long term cost, functions as an average of all the short term costs summed over some period of time.
# Scaling
We will denote $\Delta\text{CTP}$ as the cost of production.
1. Economies of increasing growth on scale, are those where $$\Delta\text{CTP}\downarrow\Rightarrow\Delta Q\uparrow$$
2. Economies of constant growth on scale, are those where $$\Delta\text{CTP}=0\Rightarrow\Delta Q\uparrow$$
3. Economies of decreasing growth on scale, are those where $$\Delta\text{CTP}\uparrow\Rightarrow\Delta Q\uparrow$$
