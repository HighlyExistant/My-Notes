This is represented by a [[Set Theory|set of objects]] together with a [[Relations|binary relation]] usually denoted $\to$ called a reduction relation, or a ==**rewrite relation**==, or simply just reduction.
# Notation
* $\overset{+}{\to}$ is the [[Relations#Transitive Closure|transitive closure]], with converse $\overset{+}{\leftarrow}$
* $\overset{*}{\to}$ is the [[Relations#Reflexive Transitive Closure (Smallest Preorder)|reflexive transitive closure]], with converse $\overset{*}{\leftarrow}$
* $\overset{+}{\leftrightarrow}$ is the [[Relations#Symmetric Closure|symmetric closure]].
* $\overset{*}{\leftrightarrow}$ is the [[Relations#Reflexive Transitive Symmetric Closure|reflexive transitive symmetric closure]].
# Reducibles
An object $x\in A$ is called ==**reducible**== if there exists some other $y \in A$ and $x \to y$. otherwise it is called an ==**irreducible**==.
# Normal Forms (Irreducibles)
an object $y$ is called a normal form if $x \overset{*}{\to} y$ such that $y$ is irreducible.
