Simple harmonic motion is expressed in systems which display sinusoidal behavior in their movement, like oscillation or repetition. This can the motion of a spring, or the swinging of a pendulum. 

## Spring Hook System
The solution to this system is of the form:
$$
	x=Acos(\omega t+\phi)
$$
where:
* $A$: Amplitude of the spring, or how far it goes on either side. Could also be referred to as the displacement of the mass.
* $\omega$: Is the frequency, or how fast it goes to either side.
* $\phi$: Is a phase shift, saying at which phase the spring starts in.
