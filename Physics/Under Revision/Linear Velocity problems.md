1. A trolley is moving at a constant velocity of $50m/s$, if there was a human tied to the tracks $1400$ meters away. 
	* how long would it take for the train to run over them?
#### Solution
If velocity is **distance** over **time** and what we're trying to do is find at what time the distance equals $1400$, we can solve for the equation:
$$
	50s=1400
$$
where $s$ is the amount of seconds and $50$ is the speed at which the trolley is going. Solving for $s$ we get $s=28$.
