* $\overset{\to}{x}$: Represents a position vector.
* $d$: Represents a distance scalar.
* $\overset{\to}{A}$ and $\Delta \overset{\to}{x}$: Represents a displacement vector.
* $v$: Represents a speed scalar.
* $\overset{\to}{v}$: Represents a velocity vector.
* $\overset{\to}{a}$: Represents an acceleration vector.
