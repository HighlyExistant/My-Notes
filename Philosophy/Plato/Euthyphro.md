# Main Characters
* Euthyphro
* Socrates
# Vocabulary
* ==**Neologist**==: A proponent of a new doctrine.
* ==**Converse**==: a situation, object, or statement that is the reverse of another or corresponds to it but with certain terms, ==**transposed**==.
# Context
* Socrates is awaiting his trial of impiety
* Euthyphro is involved in a legal case against his father who had slain one of their domestic slaves in Naxos.
# Points Made
* The Socratic Question: "What is piety".
