# Summary
As Theodorus praises his student Theaetetus, Socrates gets interested in him and asks him the question ==Is not learning growing wiser about that which you learn==. He proposes that
* Men are wise in that which they know
* Wisdom and knowledge are then the same
Theaetetus agrees, but Socrates interjects with his Socratic question: ==What is Knowledge?==
Theaetetus's initial proposition is that knowledge is:
* Sciences they learn is knowledge
* The Geometry he learns
* The art of the cobbler and other craftsmen
Socrates opposes by saying he wants to learn the abstract concept of knowledge, not the things inside of knowledge, such as the arts.
