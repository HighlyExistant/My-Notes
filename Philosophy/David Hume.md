# Consciousness of Hume
Hume says that our ability for thought is dependent on our experiences. We can only imagine that which we have seen. Those things we imagine that don't exist then only exist due to the meshing of different ideas.
# Causality
Is the ability of an object to affect the change. Hume states that this ability for knowing causality comes from repetition, as the first time we see something happen we can't know if its a one off thing, but as we experience something repetitively we can see that one thing has such a power to cause some change. Hume states that when someone says ==**This causes that**==, you are saying that ==**in all instances of this happening it always leads to that**==.
