# Types of Power

## Repressive Power
This is the traditional way of viewing power, in which the ability of a group is restricted in some way, explicitly. This is the idea of using ==**violence**== as a method of power, such as the conquering of a country, or sentencing a criminal to jail. While this power can be seen as effective, it also implies a failure of some kind that lead to the need to use this power, such as the breaking of a law.
## Normalizing Power
Is the type of power in which through no external force and by their own will, the person does as society wishes them to do. This can be like not stealing at a farmers market, the honor code, etc. If we are taught not to steal, then we will not steal, if we learn the value of a diploma, then we will seek a university. ==**It is the power that determines what we see as normal**==. It is the power to implant ideas and make one believe they are their own, which in some way, could be true. Foucault says that we are all very largely influenced by the society we are a part of, and without a society you wouldn't really be a person. Everyone in some way will be normalized. Ej. Universities, commercials, family, hospitals, psychiatric clinics, science, etc.
