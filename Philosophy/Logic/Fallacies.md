* ==**Non Sequitur**==: Latin phrase for, "**It does not follow",** a conclusion or statement that does not logically follow from the previous argument or statement.
* ==**Tu QuoQue**==: Tries to discredit an argument by attacking an opponents own behavior (accusation of hypocrisy).
* ==**Ad Hominem**==: Tries to discredit an argument by attacking the opponents character.
