# Anatomy
## Vulva
* ==**Pubic Bone**==: Found in the pelvic girdle, it is the front part of the bone, where the arches meet.
* ==**Mons Pubis (Monte de Venus en español)**==: Is located over the pubic bone, and precedes the vulva.
* ==**Labia Majora**==: Is what follows the mons pubis and surrounds the internal structure of the vulva.
* ==**Labia Minora**==: The two flaps of skin that are part of the internal vulva.
* ==**Vestibule**==: Contains the ==**urethral**== and ==**vaginal opening**==.
* ==**Clitoris**==: It's main purpose is for sexual pleasure.
* ==**Bartholin's Glands**== and ==**Skene's Glands**==: Help lubricate the vaginal opening during arousal.
## Internal Reproductive System
* ==**Vagina**==: Is what follows from the vulva's vaginal opening.
* ==**Cervix**==: Is the part between the uterus and the vagina. It also helps produce ==**discharge**== to clean the vagina.
* ==**Uterus**==: What follows from the cervix. This is the part where the fertilized egg is implanted, and where the uterus will stretch to grow the baby when pregnant. This is also where the uterine lining is where the blood from menstruation comes from.
