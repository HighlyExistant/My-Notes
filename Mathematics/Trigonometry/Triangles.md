# Area of a Triangle
## Using Cross Product Length
Given lengths $b, c$ and angle $\alpha$ formed by both sides, you can obtain the area as: $$\frac{1}{2}bc \sin(\alpha)$$The cross product is halved to give you the area of a triangle, since originally it is the area of a parallelogram.
## Heron's Formula
With sides $a, b, c$
$$
	A=\sqrt{s(s-a)(s-b)(s-c)}
$$
$$
	s=\frac{1}{2}(a+b+c)
$$
