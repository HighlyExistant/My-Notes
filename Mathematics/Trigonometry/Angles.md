# Angle Measurements
* ==**Revolution**==: Uses the symbol $\text{rev}$ Is defined as a full rotation in a circle
* ==**Degrees**==: Uses the symbol $\circ$ where $360^{\circ}$ is equal to $1$ $\text{rev}$.
* ==**Radians**==: Is the radius of the circle mapped onto its circumference. $\pi=180^{\circ}$.
# Arc Length
To calculate the arc length of a circle given a radius $r$ and angle $\theta$, then $$L=\theta r$$
This is given due to the fact that radians use the radius of a circle to denote how much an angle has rotated. $\pi$ being the amount of times a diameter fits in a circle, makes a full rotation equal to $2\pi$ radians.
# Arc Area
The arc area is simply the integral of the arc length $$\int{\theta r}= \frac{1}{2}r^{2}\theta$$
# Reference Angles
A reference angle is a way to represent any angle on the 4 quadrants of the cartesian coordinates in such a way where it is within $0^{\circ}-90^{\circ}$. The point of reference of these angles is the line at the start of the quadrant once you enter.
## Complementary Angles
Any 2 angles that sum to $90^{\circ}$ are complementary 

## Formulas for the Sum and Difference of Angles
For $\text{sin}$:
* $\text{sin}(\alpha + \beta)=\text{sin}(\alpha)\text{cos}(\beta)+\text{cos}(\alpha)\text{sin}(\beta)$
* $\text{sin}(\alpha - \beta)=\text{sin}(\alpha)\text{cos}(\beta)-\text{cos}(\alpha)\text{sin}(\beta)$
For $\text{cos}$:
* $\text{cos}(\alpha + \beta)=\text{cos}(\alpha)\text{cos}(\beta)-\text{sin}(\alpha)\text{sin}(\beta)$
* $\text{cos}(\alpha - \beta)=\text{cos}(\alpha)\text{cos}(\beta)+\text{sin}(\alpha)\text{sin}(\beta)$
For $\text{tan}$
* $\text{tan}(\alpha + \beta)$
## Formulas for Doubling Angles
using the formulas of sum and difference if $\alpha=\beta$
For $\text{sin}(2\theta)$:
* $2\text{sin}(\theta)\text{cos}(\theta)$
For $\text{cos}(2\theta)$
* $\text{cos}^2(\theta)-\text{sin}^2(\theta)$
## Formulas for Half Angles
* $\text{cos}(\frac{\alpha}{2})=\pm \sqrt{\frac{1+\text{cos}(\alpha)}{2}}$
* $\text{sin}(\frac{\alpha}{2})=\pm \sqrt{\frac{1-\text{cos}(\alpha)}{2}}$
* $\text{tan}(\frac{\alpha}{2})=\pm \sqrt{\frac{1-\text{cos}(\alpha)}{1+\text{cos}(\alpha)}}$ or $\frac{1-\text{cos}(\alpha)}{\text{sin}(\alpha)}$ or $\frac{\text{sin}(\alpha)}{1+\text{cos}(\alpha)}$
