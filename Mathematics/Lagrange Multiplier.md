# Solving constrained optimization using tangency
The following are some notes I took on [This khan academy video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yuqB-d5MjZA&list=PLCg2-CTYVrQvNGLbd-FN70UxWZSeKP4wV)

To maximize a function $g(x, y)$ on a space $f(x,y)$, we must find the point when their two slopes are proportionally equivalent, described as:
$$
\nabla g(x, y)=\lambda\nabla f(x, y)
$$
where $\nabla$ is the [[Derivatives#Gradients|gradient]] of the function, and the $\lambda$ is the proportionality constant, to set them both as proportional to each other.
