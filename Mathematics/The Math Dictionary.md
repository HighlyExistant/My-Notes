#### $a$ Is Proportional To $b$
$$a \propto b$$
Written for when the change of one variable $a$ is proportional to the change of the variable $b$. For example, the radius of a circule and the arc length of a circle are proportional to one another. As one grows, the other one also grows proportional to it. Two functions $f(x)$ and $g(x)$ are proportional, if their ratio $\frac{f(x)}{g(x)}$ is a constant function ([retrieved from wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Proportionality_(mathematics))).
#### Normalized
Means "having unit length", or the magnitude of it being equal to $1$, the [[Algebraic Structures#Identity|multiplicative identity]].
