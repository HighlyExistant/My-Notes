# What is the study
Here I will do a study on 5x5 squares with 5 colors which do not repeat per row, and rows which do not repeat patterns. In total there are $5!=120$ total combinations one can do with these colors per row. We will denote each of these $5$ colors as $\alpha, \beta, \gamma, \delta, \omega$. There are then a few ways we can go about representing this:
## Sets
We can represent this as the set $X$ of ordered pairs $X_i$ such that $X_i$ is a tuple of $5$ values, and $X$ has length $5$. One example of $X$ can be: $$X=\{(\alpha, \beta, \gamma, \delta, \omega), (\omega, \alpha, \beta, \gamma, \delta), (\delta, \omega, \alpha, \beta, \gamma), (\gamma, \delta, \omega, \alpha, \beta), (\beta, \gamma, \delta, \omega, \alpha)\}$$
when the order of the rows does not matter.
## Square Matrix
We can represent it as a $\text{5x5}$ square matrix, of distinct variables: $$\begin{bmatrix}\alpha & \beta & \gamma & \delta & \omega \\ \omega & \alpha & \beta & \gamma & \delta  \\ \delta & \omega & \alpha & \beta & \gamma  \\ \gamma & \delta & \omega & \alpha & \beta \\ \beta & \gamma & \delta & \omega & \alpha\end{bmatrix}$$
# Can you make a colored checkered grid without similar neighbors
Let a checkered colored grid be a $\text{5x5}$ square matrix $M$. A neighbor of $M_{ij}$ (column major) is defined as any element in $M$ which is $M_{(i\pm 1) j}$ or $M_{(i\pm 1)(j\pm 1)}$. The reason why it cant be $M_{i (j\pm 1)}$ is because we already defined that previously to be impossible. We denote these neighbors of $M_{ij}$ as a new matrix called $\text{3x3}$ $M_{ij}^N$, and we define similar neighbors to be those neighbors with the same color. In total there are $6$ neighbors, meaning that by the [[Proofs#Pigeonhole Principle|pigeonhole principle]], there will be $1$ similar neighbor. We also have to ensure that none of these neighbors are the same neighbor, therefore the best possible outcome would be that given a total of five colors, we have two repetitions of colors. Therefore we just have to ensure that of those $6$ neighbors, the two similar neighbors are on opposite diagonal vertices and that will ensure the rows are both disimilar and with nonrepeating neighbors.
