I seem to have acquired two opposing ways of writing my ideas on ***things***, where it is either an analytical study of absolutes, or a fuzzy understanding of what could be considered linguistic games of perhaps, subjective topics. This is of course natural, as it is hard to talk about certain topics, without introducing an unlimited amount of edge cases which need to be filled, and when talking about topics which aren't absolutes, there will always be an edge of probability which cannot be completely stomped out. We are riddled with assumptions which can ultimately lead to drastic erroneous conclusions. But what choice have I to continue down the path of an analyst, and be lost in the edge cases which need to be constantly covered, or to instead delve into poetry and fear it be misunderstood. It is the constant fight between objective and subjective, fact and opinion, reason over emotion. It is here where dichotomies arise and where I find that they are limiting in their material black and white material. So even in the analytical point of view, we can see that it is not perfect, and therefore, only a fusion with the analytic and synthetic can get us anywhere. It is why we have their distinction, and why we continue to use it. Nowhere do I see the misuse of "facts over emotion" more than in modern debates, wherein one side will use the classic, "facts don't care about your feelings", just to trample on a good point, for their harmful one. At best it is enforcing the stereotype that emotions are secondary to us, and at worst it is pushing harmful rhetoric that actively harms us as a society.
# A Fusion of the Dichotomies
This is the reason I fuse my approaches. Most facts in this world are in some way subjective to the people who experience them. Sometimes the best approach to a problem, is the one where everyone's emotional needs are met, sometimes they aren't. It's necessary to have all these contrasting points of view to help make better decisions. Although this does lead to the next issue which is that, if everyone has a seat at the table, your bound to have someone saying the most nonsensical things, and another eating it up. While on the other hand, if you exclude people from the table, who is to say you would not also be excluded.
