What is my biggest fear. Even though I have a wide range of fears, which ripple through the thoughts of my subconscious, constantly dissipating and reappearing, as waves of events course throughout my life; I feel as though one controls my very being and makes it so that I become a slave to my own mind. It is the thought of making someone I love uncomfortable. I try my best to make everyone I love, share a safe space with me, but I fear they misunderstand my intentions. It is as if the very world is against my actions, when I partake in them, and in my head, they assign meanings which don’t exist. For inside my culture, my actions are done in a different context, and they mean an entirely different thing. Perhaps I no longer belong to the culture I once regarded myself as a part of, and it is my past self which retains the previous context and tries to view it in that previous psyche. It is as if my mind has fractured between what I once knew and what I know now to be my truth. And my definitions, still with their contradictions, feel more justified as I have viewed them with a skeptic's eyes and pinned them with an analyst's intuition. As I assign meaning to my actions, others assign their own meaning, within their own context of how they view me. 

Perhaps that is my biggest fear, the thought of being misunderstood. I wish to understand what you think I meant when I said my words to you. That when I spoke them, you heard them how I did, and understood them, how I did, and that you, like myself, could agree on the grounds that we love each other. I want my words to speak truth, and for them to be impossible to twist. For the words I use to be understood by the few who dare to understand what it is I write, not in forms of subjective interpretation, but through reason. I want my words, when genuinely spoken from me, to be understood on the grounds that I would not just lie to make one feel better. 

The thought that when I say something, it is just to say it, not to mean it, is my biggest fear. I am not the dictator of truth, nor am I the most important voice in the room. Nonetheless I want to be a reliable voice, from which my judgements can be taken seriously, and not with pretentious snobbery. I wish for my compliments to not be ignored and my critiques to be taken to heart. I want the people to see what I see in them, even if I am biased. In some way, I want my thoughts to be naked, without any packed meaning aiming to deceive. For my actions to further mean what I mean when I say what I mean. 

And I suppose not being believed is what I fear the most. I, who try to put as much meaning to what I say and try my best not to lie. For when I am not believed it is as if I have become a very abject of discourse. My words, no longer carrying the same weight as I perceived them to have, as I realize they join the air, forgotten and misinterpreted. Or worse, never acknowledged to begin with. Because why would I believe someone who likes me, they who are biased, and whose reasonings for liking will reflect that. And how contradictory and stupid that statement is. Of course, I will be biased towards you because I like you, but that initial liking never began from a sense of bias. It came from your actions, and how I perceived them, as well as the meaning I saw in them. It was a subject of discourse from which your arguments, your actions for being liked, whether never stated nor acknowledged by you, were seen either way. And as a case pleading in front of my eyes, one that none of us knew was even happening, occurred; we reached a conclusion of mutual liking. So why is it that my bias will affect my better judgement, when that judgement was already reached previously without bias. And if so, who cares about this bias they have towards you. It is nothing but a judgement pre-fetched, acknowledged and further restated in every action taken towards you. And what if that judgement that you share was not reciprocated. 

This is why my biggest fear is to be rejected. Rejection is a feature built into the very life we live, and so it is very selfish of me to not want to be rejected. For me to love someone, when in reality they only liked me. For me to think someone is so smart, for them to think I was rather stupid. For my words, which fail to describe the exact meaning I portray, as they limit my sense of expression, to not be taken seriously. For my actions to be misinterpreted on the basis that the meaning I assigned to them has little significance in the larger context constructed around me. And for the very people I love to reject me, after an action I took, which I did not know would lead to this outcome.
