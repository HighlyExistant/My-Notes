I am very ill tempered when I meet someone who does not know what I know, even if I try to portray myself otherwise. I wonder what could lead someone to have such differing views, views that, at least to me don't make all too much sense. It is not that everyone should know what I know, but more like, an astonishment at the difference in our conclusions. This difference of conclusions of course is derived from a difference of the inputs from which we grew up with. What you know is a combination of your human DNA, your nature, as well as the events that occurred in your life, your nurture. Not everyone has loving parents, and not everyone's parents are born into riches.
