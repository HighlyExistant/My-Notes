# Clarifications
* ==**Ahh ok**==: You've cleared up a misunderstanding I had, which was confusing me heavily, I now understand what you mean, and I'm grateful for your explanation.
* ==**Oh ok**==: You've cleared up a misunderstanding I had, I now understand what you mean, Thank you.
* ==**Makes sense**==, ==**That's understandable**==, ==**Reasonable**==: I understand how you feel, and want to let you know that I am reading your texts and not just skimming them.
# Funny Synonyms

* ==**Perchance**==: Perhaps (but funny)
