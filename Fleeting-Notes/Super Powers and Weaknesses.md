This is a more lighthearted note for ideas about superpowers and what their weaknesses and limits would be, or which ones I impose unto them. Highly subjective. Some of the vocabulary to be used is:
1. ==**The User**==: Person with the powers
2. ==**Superhero Land**==: Magical place where we can alter things simply because of some unknown force entity etc that we ourselves invent.
3. ==**Area**==: I use the term area very loosely here, but it refers to any n-dimensional area of the universe.
# Time Manipulation Powers
These are all the powers related to in some form, time manipulation. Within them there is: 
1. Time dilation
2. Time stop: Or just the extreme version of time dilation.
3. Time Reversal
4. Time Acceleration
Or a fusion of these all at once.
## Time Stop
The Ability to stop time, comes with it, a few caveats and limitations, depending on what you write is to be able to do. A few of these questions are:
1. ==**When time is stopped, can the user think while in it?**== If not then the plot cannot progress any further and therefore ==**most people would say yes the user can think while in time stop**==. We will call this ==**the consciousness condition**==, such that true means they can think and false means they cannot. But if not, and you still would want to progress the plot then a few other questions need to be asked, namely:
	- ==**How long does the ability to stop time last?**==. If the consciousness condition is true, then this is pretty useful, as it can basically be equivalent to having very good reflexes, since a person who can pause and ponder, for even a few seconds, can instantly gain seemingly good reflexes, even if they cant move. If however the consciousness condition is false, then its almost as if nothing happened at all, as the ability lasting 10 years or even a thousand will hold little meaning to someone who cant feel it. It's almost like not having the ability at all. The time the ability lasts will be called its ==**duration**==. It also leads to the question of ==**how long is the cooldown of the power?**== If it even has a cooldown. If it doesn't have a cooldown, then the ability can be used constantly, and therefore the duration doesn't really matter all that much, if the user consistently remembers to call it again.
	- ==**Does the person age while using the ability?**==: If the consciousness condition is true, then there will always be a difference between the physical age of the person and the mental age of the person. Realistically the nervous system would still be in action while the ability is active, and therefore the person would age naturally. However due to the fact we're in *superhero land* we can adjust it so that the person doesn't need to age physically while in it, only mentally. If the ability is in sync with their mental and physical ages, that means that while in time stop you age naturally, we'll call their ages ==**adjusted to the duration**==. We'll call ==**in universe time**==, the time spent for all other subjects that don't have time stop. If they don't age while in time stop, then they are ==**synced with in universe time**==.
	- ==**Does the person remember what happened during their ability?**==: This is an alternative to the previous question, where one can opt out of having their mental ages fall out of sync with their in universe time, by simply resetting their memory after each use, making it so that they can change whatever needs to be changed, and adjusting back to how they were. This can have the ugly consequence however that if you are adjusted to the powers duration, then you wont remember what you did, but you will still age.
2. Now that we've discussed the consciousness condition, we can move onto the ==**area condition**== where we ask if the powers are constrained to a specific place in time, or not. The simplest answer is that it affects the entire universe, in which case we can say the power is of ==**universal scale**==, but if not, we can say it is of ==**local scale**==:
	* ==**If on local scale, how big is the area of the ability?**==: If the ability can cover the whole universe, then again it is simply universal scale and this question doesn't really matter, but if it does then it can lead to weird time manipulation shenanigans. It also leads to the question of whether the ==**area is fixed**== or ==**does it have a maximum and a minimum area it can be?**== We can also ask ==**what the shape is the area?**== whether it is spherical, cubic, amorphous and changing, etc.
	* ==**Is the abilities origin constrained to the user or can its origin be changed?**== If the origin is mutable, it also leads to the question of ==**how far can the origin be moved?**==
	*  ==**Can others move while in time stop?**==: If they can, than do the same rules in these questions apply to them as well, or do they experience time differently due to their own set of powers. ==**Or are these rules constant for all powers**==.
### The Implications of Time Stop
This is a listing of the implications of having time stop powers, and ways of logically formulating reasons of having time stop.
#### Formulation 1: Super Speed
It is 'possible' to stop time, if you were to travel at the speed of light, so then the question becomes, ==**do you have super speed allowing you to travel as fast as light (Also under the assumption that you don't die because of this)?**== which comes with it, its own array of questions, which should be discussed separately.
#### Formulation 2: Magic
Nothing needs to be super complicated, just explain it through magic.
