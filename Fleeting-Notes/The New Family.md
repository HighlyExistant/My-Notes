I never liked how your are born into your family. They choose your name, your culture and affect who you will grow up to be. It is, in my opinion, the responsibility of a parent to make their child independent, and self reliant, to the best of their capabilities. Family, that is the people you are first born with and who teach you how to act, are the first teachers you have in this world. I would go as far as to say that family are the most important teachers you will have early on in life. That said, family is a lot like a lottery. Whoever you end up with, is heavily dependent on a chaotic series of factors, which affect drastically your development, not only genetically, but also in the sense of nurturing. In some way I am presenting the classic nature vs nurture debate, with the focal point being the family. Here is where the concept of the new family comes in, which is a misnomer on my part, as it is not new. It is simply me suggesting that friends are better than family, and how we can attribute the same characteristics found in family to friends. The only thing stopping us is our culture.

My main problems with family, as I have listed so far is that
1. It is an arbitrary relationship which you must learn to deal with.
2. It can provide an insular environment for a developing child, which will lead to them carrying the same mentality as their parents, which in some cases, can turn a child into simply a carcass for their parents control.
3. It is treated as a grander relationship, than others, when it should be unbiased.
