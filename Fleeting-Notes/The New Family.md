I never liked how you are born with your family, unable to choose whether you stay with them or not, and usually have to learn to live with them forever. It is as if you are with your family, not because you want to be with them, but because you have to be with them. Here is where the concept of the new family comes in, which is a misnomer on my part, as it is not new. It is simply me suggesting that friends are better than family, and how we can attribute the same characteristics found in family to friends. The only thing stopping us is our culture.
