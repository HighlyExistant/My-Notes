Is the study of morphemes:
* ==**Lexeme (Lexical Item)**==: Minimal unit of words. It is unpredictable from the composition of its parts.
* ==**Lemma**==: The base word, that you would most likely find in the dictionary.
* ==**Morphemes**==: Minimal unit of language that cannot be divided further. (free of fixes).
  Example: *Cats* can be divided into *Cat-s*, where *cat* is the smallest word possible, and *-s* means that it is plural.
## Types of Morphemes
* ==**Free Morpheme**==: A morpheme that can be by itself, ej. *Dog* or *House*
* ==**Compound**==: Two or more free morphemes combined together (either via a space, a hyphen or together), ej. *Doghouse*.
* ==**Bound Morpheme**==: Affixes of a word:
	* Suffix: At the end of the word
	* Infix: In the middle of a word
	* Prefix: Before the start of a word
	* Circumfix: In the start and the end of a word.
