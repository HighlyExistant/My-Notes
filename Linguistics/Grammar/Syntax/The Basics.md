Syntax is used to express relations between the words.
* ==**Subject**==: a person or thing that is being discussed, described, or dealt with. ^55a171
* ==**Verb**==: Action.
* ==**Object**==: Noun or phrase receiving the verb's action.
	* ==**Direct Object**==: Noun receiving the verb's action.
* ==**Predicate**==: Containing the verb and object stating something about the [[#^55a171|subject]]. ^641e52
* ==**Constituents**==: Group of words that function as a single unit within a hierarchical structure.
The order of construction is:
Morphemes $\to$ Words $\to$ Constituents $\to$ Sentences
