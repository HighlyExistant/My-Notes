## Plot
Again to Mrs. Saville, location unknown, 1 month later on August 5th, 17--. An accident occurred on July 31st, 14 days after their last letter. They were almost captured by the ice and the fog become thicker. Around 2 o'clock however it had cleared. They encountered a human being on a sledge alongside a single dog. The sailors tried to convince them to climb aboard, where they addressed them in "perfect English, although with a foreign accent". Their limbs were frozen, his body emaciated, fatigued and suffering. The stranger recovers, but they are filled with grief and are trying to find someone who abandoned them.

It is then, 8 days later on August 13th that they write that their affection for this stranger grows. "He is gentle yet wise. His mind is cultivated, and he speaks with great eloquence", says Robert. The stranger speaks about how he has lost everything and cant start life anew.

6 days later on August 19th, Robert says that the stranger told him "You may easily perceive, Captain, that I have suffered great misfortunes. I had determined at one time that the memory of these evils should die with me, but you have convinced me otherwise. You seek for knowledge and wisdom, as I once did. I hope that the gratification of your wishes may not be a serpent to sting you, as mine has been. I do not know if a description of my disasters will be useful to you, but I know this: you are pursuing the same course and exposing yourself to the same dangers that have made me what I am. Perhaps you may be able to deduce an apt moral from my tale.", the stranger waits for one event only now, what I presume to be death, and Robert wishes to jot these down in the strangers own words as well as they can.

# Chapter 2
## Definitions

## Characters

Loved books of chivalry and romance, as well as tales of knightly adventures, with such heroes as Roncesvalles and the Round Table of King Arthur, and the Crusades.

## Plot
We can tell that Victor is a scientist at heart, who really wants to understand the laws of nature and the world he resides. Although it would seem that Victor would be depressed in their studies, Elizabeth was there to help them. Her, as well as Henry. The great loves of Victor were ""science, and natural philosophy".

When Victor was 13 years old, they took a trip near Thonon to some natural hot springs. As they were in the inn, he started reading "Cornelius Agrippa" a book authored around 1486-1535, of the occult, where his father then told him not to waste time on that trash. This only furthered their love of the occult, even if the contents of the books were already disproven by modern science. He became their disciple. He was guided by terrible disproven science.

When Victor was 15 years old, they witnessed a thunderstome advance from the mountains, and it took ahold of their curiosity. A man had visited their house, excited about the stump that had been electrocuted and Victor quickly forgot about Cornelius Agrippa, Albertus Magnus and Parcelus, as they began to look into electricity and mathematics. To Victor, this was a guardian angel guiding him.
# Chapter 3
## Characters
* ==**Dr. Krempe**==: Professor at the University of Ingolstadt, of Chemistry, to Victor.
* ==**Dr. Waldman**==: Another professor at the University of Ingolstadt, of Chemistry, which substitutes Dr. Krempe when they are not available. Appears as if they are 50 years old, has a few grey hairs, although his back hairs were black. He is short but stood straight, and has a very sweet voice.
* ==**Victor Frankenstein**==: The protagonist
## Plot
When Victor was 17 years old, he went to the university of Ingolstadt. Although he had attended schools in Geneva, his father wanted him to get accustomed to other cultures. Elizabeth had a severe scarlet fever, and while she managed to survive, their mother had caught the disease and it led to her demise. After witnessing this death, Victor was distraught.

Victor delayed his leave to University as he felt it was a sin to leave so soon after their mothers passing. Henry Clerval tried to convince his father to go to the same university as the protanist, but failed, as his father didnt see the point in it.

When Victor finally entered University Dr. Krempe tells them that the authors they had read on the occult, were useless, and he must learn everything again.

One day Dr. Waldman gives a lecture about the history of chemistry. Unlike Dr. Krempe, Waldman had a fascination for the old masters and thanked them for their work, as without them, we wouldn't be where we are now. Although he admits that their attempts at an elixir of life and transmuting metals was foolish, they are still thankful. We now know to Victors last name to be Frankenstein. He talks to themselves, convinced they will pioneer chemistry as a whole, and discover unknown powers.

Victor couldn't sleep and visited Waldman. He told the same stories he had to Dr. Krempe, to which Waldman had listened proudly. Waldman had left him a list of books to read, just like Dr. Krempe did, but this time Victor was more eager.
# Chapter 4

## Plot
Ever since their encounter with Dr. Waldman, Chemistry and natural philosophy is all they think about. They read the reccomendations of both Waldman and Krempe. For Victor, Waldman had guided their path towards knowledge, and while a slow learner, they came to understand more and more as time went on. His passion for science impressed both student and master, and for two years this was his life, not paying attention to his past in Geneva. He had even improved some chemical instruments, which led him to have more admiration in the university.

Of all the interesting subjects they encountered, the one that interested him the most was the structure and nature of the human body, and how they were endowed life. He learned about the human body, its natural decay, and spend several days and nights in charnel houses. After so long they had been struck with the secret to the cause of life, as well as developed the ability to bestow life on lifeless matter.

Victor now speaks to us directly in the 2nd person, saying that they will not tell us this secret, so we may not commit the same mistake they commited.

He spent months gathering materials in hopes of creating an 8 foot tall creature. As his passion increased his health decreased, as he became paler and lost weight. Through failed attempts they collected body parts from charnel houses, dissecting rooms and slaughter houses. He ignored family and friends.

> *No man should allow himself any pursuit that might interfere with the tranquility of his family life.
