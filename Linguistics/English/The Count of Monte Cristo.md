# Chapter 1: Marseilles - The Arrival
The story begins in the 24th of February, 1815. There is an air of uneasiness and despair. The captain Leclere died of brain fever. The way it occurred was that he was attacked by a fever that killed him 3 days later.

* M. Morrel, seems to be fond of Dantes, and is considering to promote him.
* Danglars hates Dantes, and is trying to convince M. Morrel to promote him.
* The final order of Leclare according to Danglar was to give a package to ==**Marshal Bertrand**==. Apparently he also met the emperor.
* Danglars is trying to talk ill of Dante, to convince M. Morrel to not promote him.
* M. Morrel teases Dante about his betrothed ==**Mercédès**==.
* Ultimately M. Morrel wishes to promote Dantes to captain, but he still needs to procure the other vote.
> *“That is according to the sense you attach to the question, sir. Do you mean is he a good comrade? No, for I think he never liked me since the day when I was silly enough, after a little quarrel we had, to propose to him to stop for ten minutes at the island of Monte Cristo to settle the dispute—a proposition which I was wrong to suggest, and he quite right to refuse. If you mean as responsible agent when you ask me the question, I believe there is nothing to say against him, and that you will be content with the way in which he has performed his duty.” - Dantes on Danglars*
* This portrays Dantes ultimately virtuous nature in contrast to Danglars mischievous and deceitful nature.
## Historical Context
> *If Paris had La Canebière, Paris would be a second Marseilles. - Common Saying of the time*
* It is mentioned [here](https://carfree.com/design/stwd180.html) that it can be seen as a bit of ==**parochialism**==. This street was ultimately the most important street in Marseille.
* Marseille used to be the principal port for France's colonies, which is why Dantès being a seaman here makes sense.
* In French, *La Canebière*, means **hemp field**. This place was one of the largest traders of hemp for baskets and ropes from the middle ages until the 1930s where other fibers began to be used. This is also around the time the port began to lose its popularity, but it wasn't because of this, but instead because of a series of misfortunes that hit the port
* "King Alexander I of Yugoslavia was assassinated here in 1934, and the Nouvelles Galeries store was decimated by a fire in 1938" ([source](https://uk.hotels.com/go/france/la-canebiere-marseille))
* The old port later on was destroyed by nazis during world war 2, where it needed to be reconstructed.
* It is recently starting to regain its footing.

# Chapter 2: Father and Son
* The narrator of the story talks to us as if we are the audience saying "We will leave Danglars struggling with the demon of hatred".
* Dante traverse La Canebière, took ==**the Rue de Noailles**== and entering a small house, on the left of the ==**Allées de Meillan**==.
* Dantes comes to visit his father, and spots him incredibly poor of health in his perspective
* Apparently his dad was just surprised he came without warning, but is happy.
* Dantes dad wants to know how his life is going.
* Dantes tells his dad of the misfortune that befell Leclare for his fortune of a promotion.
* Dantes dad is clearly weak, he is happy but falls back.
* Dantes sees that his dad doesn't have money, and asks what happened, apparently he payed his debt to ==**Caderousse**==.
* Apparently Dantes dad has lived 3 months on 60 francs (in perspective 1 Franc in 1800s is about $6.20 in modern times so 60 francs is $372)
* Dantes seems to be angry at Caderousse as he enters, but attempts to conceal it.
* Caderousse seems to be greedy.
* Dantes dad blesses his son to go visit his betrothed, he calls his wife
* Caderousse talks ill of Mercédès.
* Dantes said he has better opinion of woman than Caderousse, and ignores him.
* Danglars apparently was the one who asked Caderousse to go talk to investigate whether he talked of his promotion.
* Danglars tells him to spill the tea on Dantes and his betrothed.
> *Well, every time I have seen Mercédès come into the city she has been accompanied by a tall, strapping, black-eyed Catalan, with a red complexion, brown skin, and fierce air, whom she calls cousin. - Caderousse to Danglars*
