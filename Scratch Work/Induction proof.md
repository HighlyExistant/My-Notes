 Show by induction that $$S(n)=1 + 3 + 5 + ... + 2n-1=n^2$$
### Proof
Step 1. Prove that the base case is true:
$$
	2(1)-1=1
$$
Step 2. Assume that $S(k)=k^2$
Step 3. Prove that the statement holds for the next value in $k$ also known as $S(k+1)=(k+1)^2=k^2+2k+1$
$$
	k^2+2(k+1)-1=(k+1)^2
$$
$$
	k^2+2k+1=(k+1)^2
$$
Through factorization:
$$
	(k+1)(k+1)=(k+1)^2
$$
Thus concluding the proof
