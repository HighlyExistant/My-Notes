# Making a Compass
You can make a very simple compass using the following steps
## Grab some hard material
here I'll be using a card, as its harder than paper.
![[Compass 1.jpeg]]
## Cut the material using a ruler
While I don't think it needs to be perfect, I do think it makes it easier to use.
![[Compass 2.jpeg]]
## Mark every unit of measurement and poke a hole
I use a skewer stick to poke a hole, but it can be with a pencil or any other thing that can poke holes on the material. ==**Be careful not to poke your hand through!**==
![[Compass 3.jpeg]]
![[Compass 4.jpeg]]
![[Compass 5.jpeg]]
And now you're done with your compass. You can use the skewer to hold it in place, and put your pencil into the holes to spin and create a circle. While it is more limited than a compass you can buy, it is better than nothing.
