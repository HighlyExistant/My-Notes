# ANTIDUALISM AND ANTIMENTALISM IN RADICAL BEHAVIORISM*
* [By José E. Burgos](https://www.behavior.org/resources/915.pdf)
## Vocabulary

* ==**Antimentalism**== is the rejection of the ontological theses that mind is causal, internal, subjective, and nonbehavioral in nature. Radical behaviorists conflate both rejections, based on depictions of mentalism as inherently dualistic. [source](https://psycnet.apa.org/record/2016-12599-001).
* ==**Antidualism**== is the rejection of ontological dualism, the partition of reality into physical and nonphysical. [source](https://psycnet.apa.org/record/2016-12599-001).
